# Blue Hallways

## ⚽ Soccer Manager

The complete way to run a soccer squad from the dugout or the couch. Players, fixtures, and season stats — all in one place.

Features:

- **Squad management** — maintain a roster of players with positions, availability, and notes, so you always know who's fit and who's starting.
- **Fixture & match tracking** — log results, keep your league position up to date, and follow the season week by week.
- **Season stats** — goals, assists, and other key numbers roll up automatically across the season.
- **Built for iPad** — a comfortable, glanceable layout designed for use in the moment, not a spreadsheet.

Available on **iPadOS**.

## 💡 Ring Light

A premium ring light, paired with the software that drives it. Engineered for even, shadow-free illumination and controlled precisely the way you want.

Features:

- **Custom hardware** — the light itself, designed around even, shadow-free illumination.
- **Companion software** — full control over settings that a standard ring light won't let you adjust.
- **A complete system** — hardware and software working together for consistent, repeatable results.

## 🧊 Cube Solver

A Rubik's Cube solver for **macOS and iOS**. Enter your cube's state by hand or scan it with the camera, and get a step-by-step solution — for both the classic **3×3** and the **2×2** pocket cube.

Features:

- **3D guided solves** — watch each move as the correct face turns, and rotate the cube to match your own view.
- **Speedcubing timer** — a full-screen stopwatch with inspection time, plus best / mean / Ao5 / Ao12 stats and full solve history.
- **Random scramble** — generate a fresh scramble and solve it instantly to practice without a physical cube.

Available on **macOS and iOS**.
