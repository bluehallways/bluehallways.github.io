A home for the projects I'm building — apps, hardware, and puzzles. Right now it covers three: an iPad soccer manager, a ring light, and a Rubik's Cube solver.

## ⚽ Soccer Manager

An iPad app for running a soccer squad from the dugout or the couch — players, fixtures, and season stats, all in one place.

Highlights:

- **Squad management** — keep a roster of players with positions, availability, and notes, so you always know who's fit and who's starting.
- **Fixture & match tracking** — log results, keep your league position up to date, and follow the season week by week.
- **Season stats** — see goals, assists, and other key numbers roll up automatically across the season.
- **Built for iPad** — a comfortable, glanceable layout you can use in the moment rather than reaching for a spreadsheet.

> **Status:** in progress

## 💡 Ring Light

A custom ring light — the hardware **and** the software that drives it, built to be more than a store-bought kit.

Highlights:

- **Custom hardware** — the light itself, designed around the goal of even, shadow-free illumination.
- **Companion software** — the app/firmware side that controls the light and exposes settings that a stock ring light won't let you touch.
- **A build-and-learn project** — the interesting part is the whole system: sensors, control, and the code that ties it together.

> **Status:** in progress

## 🧊 Cube Solver

A cross-platform app for **macOS and iOS** that helps you solve a Rubik's Cube. Enter your cube's state by hand or by scanning it with the camera, and get a step-by-step solution — for both the classic **3×3** and the **2×2** pocket cube.

Highlights:

- **3D guided solves** — watch each move as the correct face turns, and rotate the cube to match your own view.
- **Speedcubing timer** — a full-screen stopwatch with inspection time, plus best / mean / Ao5 / Ao12 stats and solve history.
- **Random scramble** — generate a fresh scramble and solve it instantly to practice without a physical cube.
