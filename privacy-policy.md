# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Blue Hallways ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in relation to our website and the applications we make.

## Short version

Our apps are designed to run locally on your device. Data such as solve times, solve history, and cube scans is processed and stored **on your own device** and is not uploaded to our servers — we do not operate a server that receives this data. This website is static and does not require an account.

## What we collect

### On-device data (Cube Solver)

The Cube Solver stores information locally on the device you use it on, including:

- **Solve history** — the times of your timed solves, including any +2 or DNF penalties you apply.
- **Computed statistics** — best time, mean, Ao5, Ao12, and total solve count, derived from the solve history above.
- **Preferences** — such as the last cube size you selected (3×3 or 2×2).

This data is used to power the Timer and History features. It remains on your device and is managed by the operating system and the app.

### Camera (Cube Solver)

When you use the camera-scanning input method, image frames from your camera are analyzed **on the device** to detect cube colors. These images are not transmitted to us, stored on our servers, or shared with anyone. Access to the camera is only requested when you actively use the scanning feature.

### What we do not collect

- We do not require or store accounts, names, or email addresses for the apps.
- We do not collect your location.
- We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for advertising.

## How we use the information

The information described above is used only to make the apps work as described — solving cubes, timing solves, and remembering your preferences on your device.

## This website

The Blue Hallways website is a static site. It does not require you to create an account and does not set personal cookies to track you. It may, however, be served through a hosting provider (such as GitHub Pages), which can automatically log standard technical information (for example, an IP address or browser type) for security and performance purposes — we do not control that logging.

## Cookies and analytics

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics on this website.

## Your choices

- You can clear the Cube Solver's local data using the app's "Clear All" control, or by deleting the app and any associated data.
- You can revoke camera access at any time from your device's system settings.
- You can disable cookies in your browser at any time.

## Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please get in touch by replying through the project repositories linked from this site.
