# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 8, 2026

Blue Hallways ("we", "our", or "us") makes apps that run locally on your device.

## The short version

Your data stays on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your data, we do not require accounts, and we do not sell or share personal information.

## What we collect

- **On your device** — app data (such as stats, history, and preferences) is stored locally and used only to make the app work.
- **Camera** — where an app uses the camera, images are processed **on your device** and never transmitted or stored by us.
- **This website** — a static site with no accounts, no tracking cookies, and no third-party analytics. It is served by a hosting provider (such as GitHub Pages), which may log standard technical information (for example, an IP address) for security and performance.

## What you can do

- Delete an app and its local data at any time.
- Revoke camera access from your device settings at any time.
- Manage cookies in your browser at any time.

## Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or how we handle your information are welcome — get in touch through the project repositories linked from this site.
